The problem

Right now placements at MBM run on WhatsApp groups, phone calls, spreadsheets and paper attendance sheets. Shortlists and venue changes get lost in chats. Attendance is taken again for every round. Students can't tell when their interview turn will come. Old placement information like CTC, rounds and common questions is spread across many files.

PlaceSync puts the whole drive in one app, from the company's first request to the offer letter, and saves each drive's data for future students.

What changed from the synopsis: the company's HR now uses the app directly. Once the TPO approves the company, HR runs their own drive in the app, following the college's rules, while the TPO can see and control everything.

Who uses it

RoleWhat they can do
TPOApproves companies and drives, sets the college's placement rules, verifies students, checks remarks before students see them, sees all offer letters, closes drives, and can do anything HR can do if a company doesn't use the app.
CoordinatorHelps the TPO on drive day: attendance, venue updates, announcements, managing the interview line. Only for the drives they are given.

InterviewerAdded by HR. Calls the next student, starts and ends interviews, writes notes, suggests select or reject. Only for their own panel.

Placed seniorShares their profile and answers common questions. Contact details are shown only if they agree.

Rules that always apply

  • HR and interviewers see only their own company's drives.
  • When a drive is closed, HR can only view it, not change it. Their access ends after a period the TPO sets.
  • Every action by HR, interviewers and the TPO is recorded, so we can always check who did what.

How people join

Companies

  1. The company fills a registration form on the company website: company details, HR details, official email, and the job they want to hire for.
  2. HR confirms the email with a one-time code (OTP). Gmail or Yahoo addresses are marked so the TPO checks them more carefully.
  3. The request reaches the TPO, who approves it, rejects it with a reason, or asks for more details.
  4. After approval, HR gets an email link and sets their own password. We never send a password by email.
  5. A company that already has an account just logs in for its next drive.
  6. If a company only sends the job details by email, the TPO can enter everything for them.

The company website starts as a few public pages in our admin website, so we don't build a second site.

Students

  1. The student signs up with their roll number and personal email, and confirms the email with an OTP. MBM has no college email.
  2. One roll number and one email can have only one account. If someone has already used a roll number, the TPO sorts it out.
  3. The student fills in branch, batch, CGPA, backlogs, 10th and 12th marks, skills and resume, and uploads marksheets as proof.
  4. The TPO or a coordinator checks the details. Only verified students can be picked for drives.
  5. After verification, CGPA and backlogs are locked. To change them, the student sends a request that is checked again.
  6. If the university gives us a sheet of student marks, the app compares it with student profiles, so the TPO only checks the ones that don't match.

How a drive runs

Draft→Waiting for TPO→Published→Ongoing→Results out→Closed

  1. HR creates the drive HR · TPO

    • Role, CTC or stipend, location, job description, bond and dates.
    • Each round with its type (pre-placement talk, online test, group discussion, technical interview, HR interview), date, time and venue or link.
    • Students can't see the drive until the TPO approves it.

  2. HR picks eligible students HR

    • The TPO sets college-wide rules first, for example whether a student who already has an offer can apply again.
    • HR filters within those rules by branch, CGPA, backlogs, 10th and 12th marks and batch.
    • At this stage HR sees only name, roll number, branch, CGPA and backlogs. No phone number, email or resume.
    • Picked students get a phone notification.

  3. Students join or skip Student

    • Every drive has a last date to respond. No response means the student skipped it.
    • Joining means the student agrees to share their resume and contact details with the company.
    • If a student joins and then misses a round without a good reason, the TPO's rules decide what happens, from a warning to being blocked from later drives.

  4. Each round Student · Coordinator · HR

    • Students get notified of the time, venue, instructions and any change.
    • Attendance: the student scans a QR code shown at the venue. The code keeps changing and works only at the venue, so it can't be shared from outside.
    • If scanning fails, a coordinator marks attendance by hand and writes the reason.
    • HR enters each student's result as selected, rejected or on hold, or uploads a results sheet when the test was on another website such as HackerRank.
    • Only selected students go to the next round. Each student sees their own result. The TPO decides whether the list of selected roll numbers is shown to everyone.

  5. Interview round and the waiting line Interviewer · Student

    • The order of the shortlist is the order of the line.
    • If several panels interview at the same time, each panel has its own line.
    • The interviewer presses Call next, then Start interview, writes notes, then End interview.
    • A student who is absent or late is moved to the end of the line and told about it.
    • Students see their place in line and roughly how long they'll wait, based on how long the interviews so far have taken. It updates without refreshing.

  6. Notes and remarks Interviewer · TPO

    • Private notes are for the company and the TPO only.
    • A remark for the student is optional. The TPO reads it first and then releases it to the student.
    • After all interviews, HR goes through the notes and makes the final select or reject decision.

  7. Final results and offer letters HR · Student · TPO

    • HR announces final results, and students are notified.
    • HR uploads one offer letter for each selected student. Only that student, the TPO and that company's HR can open it. Download links stop working after a short time.
    • The student accepts or declines the offer before a last date.
    • When a student accepts, they are marked as placed, which updates placement numbers and the TPO's rules.

  8. TPO closes the drive TPO

    • HR can now only view the drive.
    • The drive's rounds, number of students in each round, selections and CTC are saved for future batches to see.

Drive extras

Placement records
