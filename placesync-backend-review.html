Rules behind the sorting

Why a route is Heavy

  • It reads or parses files (CSV, PDF, scans)
  • It calls outside servers (company websites, webhook targets)
  • It can take more than 1–2 seconds
  • It touches thousands of rows at once (drive close, exports)

Why a route is Hot

  • Used by hundreds of students at the same moment on drive day
  • A delay is visible to everyone in the room (check-in line, interview line)
  • Load-tested before the mock drive; extra rate limits per user

Light + background

  • The change itself is saved instantly
  • Follow-ups (push to 300 students, emails, webhooks) go to the queue
  • The user never waits for those follow-ups
